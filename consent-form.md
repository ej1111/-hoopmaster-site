## Program Description

Hoopmaster is an equipment temperature monitoring service. Once
installed, sensors continuously check the temperature at your site. If a
sensor detects a reading below a safe threshold — indicating a possible
heating or equipment failure — the system automatically sends an alert
to the phone number and/or email address you provide below.

By checking the box above and signing this form, I understand and agree
to the following:

- I will receive an automated message when a monitored sensor at my
  site detects a temperature below the configured safety threshold,
  indicating a possible equipment or heating failure.
- While the condition persists, I may receive periodic follow-up
  alerts, approximately every 30 minutes, until it is resolved.
- I will receive an automated all-clear message once the condition is
  resolved.
