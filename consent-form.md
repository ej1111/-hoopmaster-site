Hoopmaster SMS Alert Program — Consent Process
Business: Ernie Johnston, sole proprietorship, doing business as Hoopmaster
Program: Automated equipment temperature monitoring alerts

How consent is collected
Hoopmaster customers enroll in the SMS alert program in person, at the time of equipment installation. Consent is collected verbally — there is no paper or web form involved in the sign-up itself. The installing technician asks the customer for a mobile phone number and reads the following disclosure aloud before entering the number into the system:

“By providing your phone number, you agree to receive SMS text alerts from Hoopmaster about your equipment's status, including temperature alerts indicating a possible heating failure. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies depending on conditions. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out of these messages, or reply HELP for help.”
The customer verbally confirms their agreement before the technician enters the phone number into the Hoopmaster system. No phone number is enrolled without this verbal confirmation.

What customers receive
Enrolled customers receive an SMS alert when a monitored sensor drops below a configured safety threshold, indicating a possible heating failure. Example message:

HOOPMASTER ALERT: FRONT zone at 33.2°F (below 35.0°F threshold). Possible heating failure — check equipment. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
Message volume is low — most weeks, zero or very few alerts are sent per customer, since messages are only triggered by an actual out-of-range reading. A follow-up "all clear" message is sent once conditions recover.

Opt-out and help
Any customer can reply STOP at any time to immediately stop receiving messages, or HELP for assistance. Consent can also be withdrawn at any time by contacting Hoopmaster directly at ejohnston516@msn.com.
