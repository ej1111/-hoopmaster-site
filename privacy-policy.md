# Privacy Policy — Hoopmaster

**Effective Date:** 9/14/2026

Johnston Construction Co ("we," "us," "our") provides the Hoopmaster equipment
monitoring service. This Privacy Policy explains what information we
collect from users of that service, how we use it, and your choices.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you enroll in the Hoopmaster monitoring service, our installation
technician collects the following information directly from you, with
your knowledge, at the time of installation:

- Your name and site/property name
- Your phone number (for SMS alerts)
- Your email address (for email alerts)
- Emergency/tech support contact preferences

The Hoopmaster device itself also collects and transmits:

- Temperature sensor readings from your monitored equipment/facility
- Device status information (WiFi connectivity, sensor health)

We do **not** collect payment card information directly (payment
processing, if applicable, is handled by a separate payment processor
under its own privacy policy).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely to:

- Send you automated SMS and/or email alerts about temperature
  conditions or equipment faults at your monitored site
- Provide customer/technical support related to your Hoopmaster device
- Maintain and improve the reliability of the monitoring service

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** share your
phone number or contact information with third parties for marketing
purposes.

We use the following third-party service providers strictly to operate
the alerting service on our behalf:

- **Twilio** (SMS delivery) — processes your phone number solely to
  deliver the text alerts you've enrolled to receive
 (email delivery)

These providers are contractually/by design limited to using your
information only to deliver the service we've engaged them for.

## 4. Message Frequency and Opt-Out

Message frequency varies based on your equipment's actual conditions —
you will only receive an alert when a monitored temperature crosses a
configured threshold, plus periodic re-alerts if a fault condition
persists, and an all-clear once resolved. Message and data rates may
apply based on your mobile carrier plan.

To stop receiving SMS alerts, reply **STOP** to any message. To resume,
reply **START**, or contact Johnston Construction Co directly, or contact your
installation technician to update your enrollment. For help, reply
**HELP** to any message or contact ejohnston516@msn.com/ 913-709-7750.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your contact and site information for as long as you remain
enrolled in the monitoring service. If you discontinue service, you may
request deletion of your information by contacting Johnston Construction Co.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including
encrypted transmission,
limited employee access, etc..

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes
will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
