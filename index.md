# Hoopmaster

**Automated equipment monitoring and alerting.**

Hoopmaster is a temperature monitoring system designed to detect
heating equipment failures before they become costly. Sensors placed
throughout a monitored space send continuous readings to a central
controller, which alerts designated contacts by SMS and email if
conditions fall outside a safe range — helping catch equipment failures
early, before serious damage occurs.
